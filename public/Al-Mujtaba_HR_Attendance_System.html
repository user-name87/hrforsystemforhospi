Shift types, leave codes & calculation rules

Discovered automatically from your schedule files' legends. Edit anything that looks off — changes are saved and reused next month.

Grace periods

Minutes of tolerance before a punch counts as late / early.

12-hour duty quota

Shifts scheduled ≥ 11.5h are treated as 12-hour duty shifts. From the (quota+1)-th worked duty shift of the month onward, the whole shift counts as overtime.

Overnight punch window

For shifts that cross midnight, how many hours past the scheduled end time to keep searching the next calendar day for a check-out punch.

Shift codes discovered

Start / End are in 24-hour HH:MM. "12h duty" shifts follow the duty-quota overtime rule; everything else follows the standard base-hours rule.

🔄 Duty Carryover

Carry over surplus duty shifts from last month so they reduce this month's quota. Shift exceptions are managed on the Exceptions page.

If an employee did more duty shifts last month than the quota, the surplus reduces this month's quota. E.g. quota=17, last month=20 → surplus=3 → this month effective quota=14. If this month they do 14, quota is met with no OT.
